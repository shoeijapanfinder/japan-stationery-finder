Japan Stationery Guide

Looking for Japanese stationery that is difficult to find outside Japan? This guide explains what to look for and how to use Japan Stationery Finder.

Important: “Japan exclusive” is not used for every Japanese product. Listings distinguish store exclusives, original/bespoke items, regional or event exclusives, collaborations, limited editions, and products that are simply hard to find outside Japan.

Japan-Exclusive Fountain Pens

Japan is a strong destination for fountain pens with retailer-original designs, limited releases, collaborations, and event editions. Check the listing’s exclusivity type and purchase channel before making a special trip.

Japanese Fountain Pen Inks

Ink is one of the most travel-friendly ways to bring home Japanese stationery. Look for store-original colors, regional themes, collaborations, and limited editions. Japan Stationery Finder currently includes specialty ink listings such as Kobe INK and other retailer-original colors.

Japan-Exclusive Stationery

Do not stop at fountain pens. Japanese stationery stores also offer notebooks, refills, stamps, collaborations, and event-exclusive items. Availability can change, so check the current status and last-checked information on each listing.

Where to Buy Japanese Stationery in Japan

Some products are available through Japanese online stores, while others are primarily physical-store items. Overseas buyers may need a proxy or forwarding service when the seller does not offer international shipping.

Tokyo, Kyoto and Kobe

When planning a Japan trip, use location filters to identify stationery worth adding to your itinerary. Kobe is especially useful for discovering Kobe INK and local store-original colors, while Tokyo has a broad range of specialty and retailer-exclusive stationery.
